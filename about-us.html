About Us

Ashvish Creative Studio is an independent creative studio making joyful things for children and families — picture storybooks, colouring books, free online tools and games, and audio stories.

Our best-known characters are Vishu and Wally, the Firefly Brothers — two brothers whose adventures are full of fun, friendship and happy endings. Vishu is the elder brother with spiky black hair and a gold earring; Wally is the younger brother in his brown hoodie, with his big headphones always on. Riding along on Wally's headphone is Sparkle, a tiny glowing firefly who spots the smallest clues.

Every Firefly Brothers story is made for fun only — no sad ends, and the culprit always becomes a friend. Our colouring pages are full, rich scenes in pure black-on-white line art, drawn fully inside the frame, so children can colour a whole little world on every page.

Our Story

Ashvish Creative Studio began with a simple idea: children learn best when they are laughing, imagining and making things with their own hands. From that idea grew a small studio that creates picture storybooks, colouring books, free online tools, playful games and audio stories — all made with care, colour and a love of good stories. Every book, game and tool we make is designed to bring a little more joy, curiosity and creativity into a child's day, whether they are reading with a parent, colouring with a friend, or playing and learning on their own.

For Parents & Teachers

Everything we make is designed to be safe, simple and fun. Our free tools and games need no sign-up, so children can play and create without accounts or fuss. Our books and stories are made to be enjoyed together — read aloud at home, shared in a classroom, or coloured in on a quiet afternoon. We keep things friendly, wholesome and easy to use, so parents and teachers can feel confident sharing them with the children in their care.

Our Founder

Ashvish Creative Studio was founded by Vishal Kashyap, a children's book author and creator who believes that stories and drawing help children learn, imagine and grow. His work brings together playful characters, gentle humour and bright, welcoming art — the same spirit you will find in every book, game and story on this website.
