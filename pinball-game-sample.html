How to play

Hold LAUNCH to pull the plunger, let go to shoot the ball. Tap the red buttons — or the left / right half of the table — to flip. On a keyboard: Z and / (or arrow keys) flip, Space launches. Keep the ball alive: if it slips between the flippers, it's gone. 3 balls per game. If the ball ever gets wedged somewhere, the table nudges it free on its own.
