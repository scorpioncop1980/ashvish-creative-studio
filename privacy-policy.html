Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit Ashvish Creative Studio, how we use it, and the choices you have. Please read it carefully. By using our website, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Introduction

Ashvish Creative Studio is a small creative studio that makes kids’ storybooks, colouring books, free online tools, games and audio stories. Our website, https://ashvishcreativestudio.com, is a place for families, young readers and young artists to read, play, create and learn.

We believe privacy should be simple. Our website is designed so that you can read our stories, use our free tools and play our games without creating an account or handing over personal information. This policy describes the limited information that may be collected when you browse our site, contact us, or order a custom portrait from us.

2. Information We Collect

We collect very little information, and most of what we do collect is provided by you directly.

Information you do not need to give us

You do not need an account to use our website. You can read our books, listen to our audio stories, play our games and use our free tools without signing up, logging in, or providing your name, email address or any other personal details.

Our free tools run entirely in your browser. Any files, text, images or other content you use with a tool on our site stay on your device and are never uploaded to our servers. We do not see, store or share the content you create with our free tools.

Information you may choose to share with us

If you contact us or order a custom portrait from us through Instagram direct message, you may share information with us, such as:

  • Your name or the name you use on Instagram.
  • The photos you send us so that we can create your custom portrait.
  • Any details you include in your message, such as your preferred art style, the people or pets to be included in the portrait, or how you would like the finished artwork delivered.

We use this information only to reply to you and to create and deliver the artwork you asked for. We do not sell it, and we do not share it with anyone else except as described in this policy.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our site and the advertising services we use may automatically collect limited technical information when you visit, such as your browser type, device type, the pages you visit, and general, non-identifying information about how our site is used. This information helps us understand which pages and tools people enjoy and how to make the site better. It is not used to identify you personally.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information described above only for the following purposes:

  • To operate, maintain and improve our website, free tools, games and audio stories.
  • To respond to your messages and to create and deliver custom portraits you have ordered.
  • To understand, in general terms, how visitors use our site so we can make it better.
  • To show advertising that helps keep our free content free, as described in the section on Google AdSense below.
  • To protect our website and our visitors, and to comply with any legal obligations that apply to us.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to anyone. We do not use the information you share with us for purposes unrelated to the ones listed here.

4. Cookies

Cookies are small text files that a website or an advertising service can store on your device. They are commonly used to remember preferences, to understand how a site is used, and to show relevant advertising.

Our website and the third-party advertising services we use may place cookies on your device. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers let you block cookies, delete cookies that have already been set, or ask for permission before a cookie is stored. Please note that if you block or delete cookies, some parts of our website or some advertisements may not work as well.

5. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

We use Google AdSense to show advertisements on our website. Advertising helps us keep our stories, tools, games and audio stories free for everyone to enjoy.

Google AdSense serves ads on this website. Google and its partners use cookies, including the DoubleClick DART cookie, to serve ads to you based on your visits to this website and other websites on the internet. The DART cookie allows Google and its partners to show you ads that are relevant to you, based on the websites you have visited.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by Google by visiting Google’s Ads Settings at https://adssettings.google.com/. You may also opt out of personalised advertising from many third-party vendors by visiting the Digital Advertising Alliance’s opt-out page at https://www.aboutads.info/choices/.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. You can choose to opt out of the use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices or by changing the cookie settings in your web browser.

Some of the advertisements on our site may also be served by other third-party advertising companies. These companies may use cookies and similar technologies to collect information about your visits to this and other websites so that they can show you advertisements about goods and services that may be of interest to you. They do not collect your name, address, email address or telephone number through these cookies.

For more information about how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services, please visit Google’s privacy and terms pages. If you would like to learn more about how online advertising works and how to control it, you can visit the Network Advertising Initiative at www.networkadvertising.org.

6. Children’s Privacy

Our content is family-friendly and is enjoyed by children together with their parents and guardians. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

Our website does not ask children to create accounts, provide their names, email addresses or any other personal information, or submit anything about themselves. Our free tools run entirely in the browser, so nothing a child creates with them leaves their device.

If you are a parent or guardian and you believe that your child has shared personal information with us, for example through a message sent to us on Instagram, please contact us through the channels listed in the Contact Us section below and tell us what was shared. We will take reasonable steps to review the message and remove the information promptly.

We encourage parents and guardians to supervise their children’s use of the internet and to talk with them about staying safe online.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us. Messages and photos you send us are used only to reply to you and to create the artwork you requested, and access to them is limited to the people at our studio who need them for that purpose.

No method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you contact us through Instagram, your messages are also subject to Instagram’s own privacy practices and security measures. Please do not send us sensitive information, such as passwords, payment card details or government identification numbers, through any messaging channel.

8. Links to Other Sites

Our website may contain links to other websites, such as our Instagram page, Amazon product pages, and other services we recommend. If you click on a link to another website, you will leave our site, and this Privacy Policy will no longer apply.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of any website that we link to. We encourage you to read the privacy policy of every website you visit before sharing any personal information with it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when we add a new feature, tool or service, or when the law changes. When we make changes, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.

We encourage you to review this page occasionally to stay informed about how we protect your information. Your continued use of our website after any changes to this policy means that you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, or if you would like us to remove information you or your child has shared with us, please contact us through our website at https://ashvishcreativestudio.com, or send us a direct message on Instagram using the Instagram link on our homepage.

We will do our best to reply to your message as soon as we can.
