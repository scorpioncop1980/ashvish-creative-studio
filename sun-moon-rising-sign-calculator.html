What do the three signs mean?

Your full birth chart has many pieces, but these three do most of the talking.

☉

Sun sign — your core

The sign the Sun was in on your birthday. It describes your basic identity, ego, vitality and the qualities you are growing into over your lifetime. This is the sign most people know as “my star sign”.

☽

Moon sign — your feelings

The sign the Moon was passing through when you were born. The Moon changes sign every ~2½ days, so it is personal to your exact birth moment. It rules your emotions, instincts, habits and what makes you feel safe.

↑

Rising sign — your mask & manner

Also called the Ascendant: the sign rising on the eastern horizon at your birth. It shapes your appearance, first impressions and automatic way of approaching life — and it sets the layout of the whole chart, which is why birth time matters.

Common questions

Why do I need my birth time?

The Rising sign completes a full circle every 24 hours, changing sign roughly every 2 hours, and the Moon moves about 13° a day. A birth time from your birth certificate or hospital record gives the most reliable result. Without it, the Sun sign is still exact and the Moon sign is right for most of the day — it can only be uncertain if you were born on a day the Moon changed signs.

Western or Vedic — which should I choose?

Western astrology uses the tropical zodiac, fixed to the seasons. Vedic (Jyotish) astrology uses the sidereal zodiac, fixed to the stars, adjusted by the Lahiri ayanamsa (about 24° today). Because of that shift, your Vedic signs are often — but not always — one sign earlier than your Western signs. Neither is “wrong”; they are two different frameworks. If you grew up reading Rashi-based predictions, choose Vedic.

My sign is different on another website. Why?

Usually one of three reasons: the other site used the other zodiac system (Western vs Vedic), your birth time or timezone was entered differently, or your Sun, Moon or Rising falls very close to a sign boundary (a “cusp”). This calculator shows the exact degree, so you can see how close to a boundary you are.

How accurate is this calculator?

Sun and Ascendant positions are accurate to a small fraction of a degree; the Moon position uses a standard astronomical series accurate to roughly 0.1–0.3°. That is more than enough to place each body in the correct sign, except in rare cases where a body sits within a few minutes of arc of a sign boundary.
