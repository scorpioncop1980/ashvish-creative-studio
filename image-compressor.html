How It Works

1

Choose your images

Click the box above or drag your photos onto the page. You can add one image or several at once.

2

Pick your settings

Choose JPEG, WebP or PNG, set the quality, and set a maximum width or height if you want smaller images.

3

Your Privacy Matters

Your images are processed on your device and are never uploaded to any server. Everything happens inside your own browser, so your photos stay private — even if you close the page, nothing is saved anywhere.
